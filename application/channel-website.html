Channel rules

Voice and visual behavior.

How FP should show up on this surface.

Voice dial.

Low-to-medium wit. Highest clarity. Questions and dry diagnosis are welcome, but the page has to convert without sounding like a thread.

Visual dial.

Editorial whitespace, sharp cards, restrained purple, viewfinder as image focus or light accent. No dark default canvas.

Rules in.

Lead with what the reader getsAsk the reader's real question before they doHeadlines under eight words where possibleUse proof and testimonials over founder narration
