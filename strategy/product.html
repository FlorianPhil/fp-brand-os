02
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not.

The filters that keep this strategic choice operational.

Rules in

Belongs

  • A guided clarity process using the Focus Star (5 edges)
  • Strategy, positioning, language, hierarchy, and creative direction
  • Outputs that help the client explain, choose, build, and brief better
  • A strategic blueprint the client can read, execute from, and return to
  • Discovery that feels therapeutic without implying clinical therapy
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Logo design
  • Content agency or social media management
  • Clinical therapy
  • Aesthetic refresh
  • Template solutions
  • Done-for-you execution without strategic ownership

04
Application

How this changes behavior.

Strategic choices that don't change behavior are descriptions, not strategy.

Strategy

position as methodology, not productized service; methodology naming rule ("the Brand Therapy process" not "at Brand Therapy")

Copy

describe Brand Therapy as a process and decision system, not an agency package; lead with clarity, confidence, and tangible direction before naming the method

Visuals

show structure, diagnosis, and clarity; avoid spa, therapy, or wellness cues; deliverables shown as documents and frameworks, not asset libraries

Sales

Discovery Call (20 min, fit + success definition + timing) → Brand Therapy Blueprint → Brand Build → Launch Ready; sales conversation IS the content

Content

every channel reinforces the methodology angle; never personify Brand Therapy as a company

05
Proof required

What evidence supports this.

Every public claim traces to verifiable evidence.

  • Service structure (Founders rate: $700; Brands rate: $5K-15K+)
  • Process steps (Discovery → Blueprint → Build → Launch)
  • Case evidence (Verdant Strategies, Cachet, Adam Freed / Freed Solutions)
  • Visible testimonials (Jon Tull of Cachet, Kevin Meredith of 4th Sector Innovations, Sam Goldman)
  • Public credentials (15+ years, 6 countries, 25+ clients/companies)
06
Output control

Rules for AI.

Directives any LLM must follow when producing content for this brand.

  • Refer to "the Brand Therapy process" or "Brand Therapy work," never "at Brand Therapy" or "the Brand Therapy team"
  • Do not imply clinical therapy or psychological treatment
  • Do not fabricate guaranteed outcomes (revenue, clients, visibility)
  • Do not claim a "proprietary framework" beyond what source approves (Focus Star)
  • Open with clarity, confidence, working blueprint: then name the method
