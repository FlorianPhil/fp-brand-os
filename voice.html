Calibration · Traits

Five Traits.

Each trait has a definition and a comparison. The "sounds like" column is the target. The "not like" column is what most brand voices default to. Knowing the gap is the whole game.

Witty
French sarcasm meets Californian lightness. Goofy when the moment calls for it, dry when it doesn't.
Sounds like
Building a brand is emotionally violent. Most people would rather just pick a color palette.
Not like
Humor is a powerful branding tool that can help you build authentic connections with your audience!
Unhurried
Observes before speaking. Lets the idea breathe. No rushing to the point.
Sounds like
Sounds simple, but it's not easy.
Not like
Here are 7 quick tips to nail your brand voice today.
Real
Sounds like a person talking. Wonders out loud. Admits not knowing.
Sounds like
Branding is really about small details. The ones nobody notices until they're wrong.
Not like
Our proven methodology has helped hundreds of founders unlock their brand potential.
Confident
Speaks from experience, not from a need to prove anything. Quiet authority.
Sounds like
You are already doing branding. It's up to you if you want to control that narrative.
Not like
Trust me, I've been doing this for 10+ years and I can tell you exactly what you need.
Nuanced
Nothing is binary. Holds multiple possibilities. Opens the perspective instead of closing it.
Sounds like
Branding sometimes is not just about what's in front of you. You have to use your gut feelings.
Not like
There are only two types of founders: those who invest in branding and those who fail.

Examples · Off-voice

Not This.

What FP copy should never sound like. Each example shows a specific failure mode. Knowing what to avoid is as important as knowing the target.

Great question! Let me help you with that. Brand strategy is all about aligning your vision with your audience's needs to create a powerful connection.
Hollow affirmation opener. Generic coaching-speak. No personality, no observation, no specificity.
In this article, we'll explore 5 proven strategies to level up your personal brand and stand out in a crowded marketplace.
Listicle energy. "Level up" is banned. "Personal brand" as lead framing. "Proven strategies" is empty.
As a seasoned brand strategist with over a decade of experience across six countries, I bring a unique perspective to every engagement.
First-chair bio. Resume voice. Florian would cringe reading this aloud at a dinner party.
However, it's worth noting that brand consistency requires a systematic approach. As a result, founders who invest in strategic clarity will see compounding returns over time.
Formal connectives ("however," "as a result," "it's worth noting"). Essay structure. Zero personality.
You've got this! Show up daily, be authentic, and watch your brand grow. The journey of a thousand miles starts with a single post.
Hustle-culture motivational post. Hollow affirmations stacked. Sounds like every LinkedIn influencer.
Brand Therapy helps founders unlock their full potential through our proprietary framework that has been refined over years of client work.
Brand Therapy as company ("helps founders"). "Unlock potential" is coaching-speak. "Proprietary framework" is consultant-speak.

06
Structure · Patterns

How It's Built.

Sentence-level and paragraph-level patterns that prevent voice drift after 500+ words. These are mechanical, not inspirational. Follow them.

Core pattern
Affirmations to questions

When tempted to state something as fact, turn it into a question the reader answers themselves. Questions invite. Affirmations close.

Rhythm
Short to anchor, long to reflect

Alternate sentence lengths. Never three long sentences in a row. The short sentence lands the thought. The long sentence lets it breathe.

Openers
Three default modes

Observation, curiosity, or admission. Never encouragement. "I noticed..." "What if..." "I got this wrong..."

Closers
Perspective shift, not lesson

End with a reframe. Leave the reader thinking, not nodding. No calls to action. No motivational send-off.

Check-ins
"Right?" as invitation

Short incomplete sentences followed by grounded explanations. Invitation, not lecture.

Duality
Switch lenses naturally

Client vs. agency, creator vs. consumer, logic vs. emotion. Find clarity in paradox. Every idea has its shadow.

Punctuation
Ellipsis over em dash

Ellipses freely for rhythm, irony, breathing room. Three dots, no space before, one space after. Colon for lists and setups.

Emphasis
Bold the load-bearing phrases

Two to four bold phrases per section in long-form. Mark what a skim reader needs. Two short sentences beat one long compound.

Channels · Adaptations

By Channel.

The voice stays the same. The volume changes. LinkedIn is peak personality. Newsletter is most conversational. Website is witty with care. Proposals dial up composure.

Channel
LinkedIn
150-300 words. Peak personality. Dad jokes earn their place here. Never end with a CTA.
Hook under 100 chars. Follow-up line that reframes. Conversational body (3-6 short paragraphs). Perspective shift ending.
Sharp observation about the industry Personal admission or confession Question that reframes a common assumption
Channel
Newsletter
500-1200 words. Most conversational channel. One idea per edition. Opens like writing to a smart friend.
Sections with bolded load-bearing phrases (2-4 per section). Can go longer, more reflective.
Something that happened this week A question a client asked An observation that has been sitting with you
Channel
Website
Headlines under 8 words. Witty with care. Smart humor that highlights value. Every section earns its place.
Lead with what the reader gets, not who Florian is. Subheads conversational.
Reframe an expectation Ask the reader's real question before they do
Channel
Proposal
As short as possible. Professional confidence dialed up. Wit is subtle, not central. Clarity and composure dominate.
Shorter paragraphs than newsletter. Every sentence does work.
Name the situation the client described Restate their words back to them

Quality Gate.

Seven questions that every piece of FP content must pass before publishing. Plus the anti-patterns that define what the voice is not.

PASS

PASS

PASS
Ends with perspective, not a lesson?
PASS

PASS
Any em dashes? Replace every one.
PASS
