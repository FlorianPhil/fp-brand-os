Copy Patterns.

Six rules govern every piece of written material. Not guidelines. Patterns. Each one produces a specific, recognizable output across newsletter, social, and web.

Pattern 01 · Purple Period
Accent-colored period at the end of every headline. Cheapest brand signal, most consistent.
Clarity is the strategy.
Pattern 02 · Two-Beat
"Not X. Y." sentence structure. Short declaratives that reframe. Reject, then redirect.
Not broken.
Unfocused.
Pattern 03 · Concrete Over Abstract
Specific numbers and details beat vague language. Always. The detail is the proof.

Pattern 04 · 12-Word Max
No headline exceeds 12 words. Constraint forces clarity. If it takes more words, it is not clear enough.
The end of the tweaking loop.

Pattern 05 · Bold Load-Bearing
Two to four bold phrases per section in long-form. Mark what a skim reader needs.
Brand Therapy is a guided clarity process. The output is a strategic blueprint the founder returns to for years. Three benefits stack: experiential, rational, emotional.
Pattern 06 · No "We Help"
Never needed. Direct statements always stronger. The work speaks for itself.
