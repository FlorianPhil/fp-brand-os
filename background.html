05
Character

How he reads.

Sharp, funny, unhurried. Professional first, but refuses to take itself too seriously.

Disposition

Observes before speaking.

Names patterns instead of cheerleading. Trusts the reader to think. Never prescriptive first. Unless enough time has been spent understanding a situation, no prescription gets issued.

Humor

Goofy when the moment calls for it. Dry when it doesn't.

Self-aware enough to make fun of the whole LinkedIn-influencer thing while doing it himself. Taking yourself too seriously is not allowed.

06
Voice mechanics

Speech patterns.

How Florian sounds in conversation, on video, on stage. These patterns inform the written voice but exist independently. They predate any brand decision.

Pace

Unhurried. Pauses before the load-bearing sentence.

Pause use

Strategic. Lets the observation land before adding to it.

Accent

French. Natural, not performed. Adds texture to English. Neither leaned into nor scrubbed out.

Sentence length

Short to anchor. Long when the idea needs room to breathe. Silence is also an option.

07
Behavior

How he shows up.

Six contexts. Each one has a default mode. The defaults are deliberate, not accidental.

In meetings

Observes first.

Asks the reframing question. Does not dominate.

On social

Observational.

Shares what he noticed, not what he accomplished.

In video

Conversational.

Direct to camera. Thinking in real time, not scripted.

In email

Short, warm, specific.

Sounds like a text from a smart friend.

On stage

Conversational keynote.

Thinks out loud with structure. Checks in with "right?" as invitation.

Boundaries

No hero framings.

No public vulnerability for content. Humor online only when it lands dry.

08
Energy in the room

Presence.

The person at dinner who makes you think and laugh in the same sentence.

  • Posture. Relaxed, open, assured. Never performative or staged.
  • Eye contact. Direct but not intense. Observes more than locks on.
  • Listening style. Notes patterns. Asks the question that reframes the conversation.
  • Default state. Curious before prescriptive. Ask the question, observe the pattern.
