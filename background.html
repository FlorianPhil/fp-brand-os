Section C · Personal Branding

Background.

The professional history, speech patterns, and behavioral signatures that make the founder recognizable.

SectionC2 StatusConfirmed SourceBrand Voice + Background
01
Track record

Professional history.

10+ years branding across 6 countries. The kind of background that makes the work global without trying to sound global.

  • 3ANGLES (Taiwan): Founded. 25+ clients including Acer, TAITRA
  • Cannabis industry: CMO role. Built brand infrastructure in a nascent market
  • 4th Sector Innovations: Brand Director
  • Renogy X: Founded the home division, built brand from zero
  • Mentored by Fabian Geyrhalter: FINIEN founder, author of "Bigger Than This"
02
Voice

Speech patterns.

How Florian sounds in conversation, on video, on stage. These patterns inform the written voice but exist independently.

  • Pace: Unhurried. Pauses before the load-bearing sentence
  • Pause use: Strategic. Lets the observation land before adding to it
  • Accent signature: French accent. Natural, not performed. Adds texture to English
  • Sentence length: Short to anchor. Long when the idea needs room to breathe
03
Behavior

How he shows up.

In meetings

Observes first. Asks the reframing question. Does not dominate.

On social

Observational. Shares what he noticed, not what he accomplished.

In video

Conversational, direct to camera. Thinking in real time, not scripted.

In email

Short, warm, specific. Sounds like a text from a smart friend.

Boundaries

No hero framings. No public vulnerability for content. Humor online only when it lands dry.

04
Energy

Presence.

  • Energy: The person at dinner who makes you think and laugh in the same sentence
  • Posture: Relaxed, open, assured. Never performative or staged
  • Eye contact: Direct but not intense. Observes more than locks on
  • Listening style: Notes patterns. Asks the question that reframes the conversation
