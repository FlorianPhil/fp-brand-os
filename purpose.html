Section A · Brand Strategy · P3

Purpose.

The goal is not more attention from more people. The goal is a brand that is easier to choose because it is less interchangeable.

Edge
P3
Name
Make every brand unique
Center link
Curiosity

Florian's work exists to make every brand unique by revealing what is already specific and turning it into decisions, not by making brands louder, prettier, or generically marketable.

Strategic question

Strategic question

  • Why does this work need to exist?
Use

Use

  • Specificity already exists in the person, work, culture, standards, or point of view.
  • Strategy should reveal and sharpen, not invent a costume.
  • Growth comes from better matches, clearer noes, and stronger fit.
  • A brand should become easier to choose because it is less interchangeable.
Avoid

Avoid

  • More marketing activity as the default answer.
  • Trend imitation.
  • Generic best-practice positioning.
  • A brand that tries to appeal to everyone.
  • Uniqueness as decoration instead of decision-making.
Proof required

Proof required

  • Examples of saying no to wrong-fit opportunities.
  • Strategy decisions that make a brand more specific and less comparable.
  • Evidence that specificity improves clarity, confidence, referrals, or conversion quality.
AI rule

AI rule

  • Do not generate generic differentiation language. Name what is uniquely true and what it excludes.
Image signal

Image signal

  • A focused selection, edited set, or clear mark of distinction. No crowd, funnel, or growth-chart cliche.
