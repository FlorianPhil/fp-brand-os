Brand Therapy

Curiosity

Brand Therapy gives clients the clarity to stop second-guessing, the confidence to make sharper brand decisions, and a working blueprint for what to say, show, sell, and build next, built through Florian's Focus Star at the intersection of brand strategy, psychology, and strategic positioning.

Strategic question

Strategic question

  • What exactly is being sold?
Use

Use

  • A guided clarity process using the Focus Star.
  • Strategy, positioning, language, hierarchy, and creative direction.
  • A client who needs a decision system, not a pile of assets.
  • Outputs that help the client explain, choose, build, and brief better.
Avoid

Avoid

  • Logo refresh as the main job.
  • Content agency or social media management.
  • Clinical therapy.
  • A template or generic brand workshop.
  • Done-for-you execution without strategic ownership.
Proof required

Proof required

  • Before/after examples where confusing value becomes easier to explain.
  • Artifacts showing the Focus Star turning into messaging, identity, or offer decisions.
  • Testimonials that mention clarity, specificity, relief, or stronger decision-making.
AI rule

AI rule

  • When generating Product copy, name the process before naming deliverables.
Image signal

Image signal

  • A focused working session, annotated strategy surface, or calm diagnostic environment. No clinical couch, no agency moodboard wall.
