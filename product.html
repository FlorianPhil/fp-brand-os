Brand Therapy. 01
Strategic decision

What is being sold.

Product defines the offer logic: what the client buys, what it replaces, and what AI must protect.

Offer name

Brand Therapy

Brand Therapy is Florian's Focus Star process for giving clients clarity, confidence, and a working blueprint for what to say, show, sell, and build next.

Client outcome

Clarity that holds

The client stops tweaking from taste, anxiety, or imitation. Decisions get a reason.

Offer category

Strategic diagnosis

The product is the Focus Star-led process that turns unclear expertise into a strategic blueprint, messaging, and creative direction the client can actually use.

02
Value stack

What clients buy.

Every Product output should show why the process matters before listing deliverables.

Emotional benefit

relief from the tweaking loop.

Business benefit

sharper positioning, proof, offer logic, and creative direction.

Practical output

a blueprint for copy, website, social, email, sales, and creative briefs.

Real test

If the work cannot help a client make a sharper decision after the session, it is decoration, not Brand Therapy.

03
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not.

These filters prevent the offer from drifting into agency, therapy, coaching, or template language.

Rules in

Belongs

  • A guided clarity process using the Focus Star.
  • Strategy, positioning, language, hierarchy, and creative direction.
  • A client who needs a decision system, not a pile of assets.
  • Outputs that help the client explain, choose, build, and brief better.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Logo refresh as the main job.
  • Content agency or social media management.
  • Clinical therapy.
  • A template or generic brand workshop.
  • Done-for-you execution without strategic ownership.
04
Marketing use

Triggers, proof, and output controls.

This is the AI-readable layer for website copy, landing pages, sales, content, visual direction, and image prompts.

Triggers

When it matters

  • Rebuilding a website, offer, or public presence.
  • Good work with messy explanation.
  • Unclear whether brand, copy, audience, or offer is the real problem.
  • Need for a system before investing in more execution.
Proof required

Show evidence

  • Before/after examples where confusing value becomes easier to explain.
  • Artifacts showing the Focus Star turning into messaging, identity, or offer decisions.
  • Testimonials that mention clarity, specificity, relief, or stronger decision-making.
AI rule

AI behavior

When generating Product copy, name the process before naming deliverables.

Output control

Copy

Explain Brand Therapy as a process and decision system. Do not make it sound like an agency package.

Output control

Visual

Show structure, diagnosis, and clarity. Avoid spa, therapy, or wellness cues.

Output control

Image

A focused working session, annotated strategy surface, or calm diagnostic environment. No clinical therapy cues, no agency moodboard wall.
