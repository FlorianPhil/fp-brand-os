Section A · P2 · Audience Intelligence

People.

The audience is not everyone with a founder title. It is the person whose work has become more specific than the market can currently read.

The Invisible Expert Wrong comparison Business differentiation Better-fit demand
The problem is not lack of visibility. The problem is wrong comparison.
01
Strategic decision

Who this is really for.

This is the audience contract for strategy, copy, design, imagery, offer decisions, and AI generation. The Focus Star gives the direction. This page turns it into usable audience intelligence.

Audience name

The Invisible Expert

People whose work is meaningfully specific, but whose brand makes them look easier to compare than they should be.

Core condition

Specific work, flat read

They have proof, taste, judgment, process, or a real point of view. The market still reads them through a generic category.

Business outcome

Be chosen correctly

The goal is clearer differentiation so the right market can understand, trust, refer, price, and choose the work with less explanation.

02
Persona

Primary profile.

This is not a demographic avatar. It is the buying psychology and business state that should shape the brand.

Inner line

"The work is not generic. The way it is explained is."

They are not asking to be more loved. They want the market to stop flattening the value they already built.

Likely role

Founder, consultant, expert, studio lead, operator, or service-business owner where the person behind the work materially changes trust and choice.

Business state

Past the beginner stage. There is real value, real work, and some proof. The next chapter requires sharper positioning before more visibility or execution.

Emotional state

Tired of explaining. Irritated by being compared to cheaper or simpler alternatives. Ambitious, but skeptical of loud personal-brand theater.

Buying belief

A better brand should reduce confusion, improve fit, protect pricing power, and make future decisions easier. It should not turn them into a character.

03
Psychographics

What they care about.

These fields are what AI needs to make better copy, imagery, page structure, and offer decisions without inventing a fake audience.

Values

Specificity

  • Depth over volume.
  • Taste with a reason.
  • Plain language that still has precision.
  • Autonomy, discernment, and adult trust.
Wants

Business clarity

  • A brand that makes the work easier to understand.
  • Better-fit referrals and sales conversations.
  • Language that protects nuance without becoming vague.
  • A visual world that feels as specific as the work.
Rejects

Performance

  • Influencer energy.
  • Hustle-culture visibility advice.
  • Generic premium branding.
  • Personality as costume.
04
Marketing use

Triggers, criteria, and objections.

This is the part that should inform landing pages, sales calls, content, email, proof, and offer framing.

Decision triggers

When they buy

  • Entering a more expensive or visible market.
  • Rebuilding a website, offer, deck, or public presence.
  • Raising prices but still sounding comparable.
  • Realizing more content will only scale the wrong message.
  • Feeling their category has become too noisy or AI-flattened.
Buying criteria

What must be true

  • Florian has to read the nuance before prescribing.
  • The process must connect strategy, language, proof, and visuals.
  • The output must be reusable, not just impressive in the moment.
  • The work must feel sharp without turning them into a performance.
Objections

What blocks them

  • "I do not want to become a personal brand caricature."
  • "I am not sure brand is the real business problem."
  • "I need execution too, not just strategy."
  • "I have rebuilt this before and still felt unclear."
05
Alternatives

What they compare against.

These are not enemies. They are the common paths the audience may consider before choosing Brand Therapy.

Category alternatives

Common paths

  • Brand studio: stronger look, but not always sharper market meaning.
  • Positioning consultant: better language, but often weak creative translation.
  • Designer or logo refresh: visible progress before diagnosis.
  • Copywriter or content agency: more output, not necessarily a clearer source.
  • DIY AI or template: faster and cheaper, but likely to reproduce category language.
  • Coach or personal brand guru: confidence and attention, not always differentiation.
Florian's angle

Diagnosis before expression

Brand Therapy wins when the buyer understands that the problem is not just how the brand looks or sounds. It is the missing bridge between the specific value of the work and the way the market currently categorizes it.

06
Creative rules

How this should look and sound.

This is where the audience strategy becomes usable for design, image generation, copy, and AI instructions.

Image brief

Quiet authority

  • One person, not a crowd or generic team.
  • Calm workspace, notes, laptop, strategic materials.
  • Editorial, grounded, observant, focused.
  • Signals competence before personality.
Avoid visually

No stock theater

  • No guru stage, hype pose, or influencer desk.
  • No clinical therapy cues.
  • No exaggerated laptop smile.
  • No chaotic moodboard wall as the main signal.
AI writing rule

Business outcome first

  • Frame visibility as a result of differentiation, not the main desire.
  • Write from wrong comparison, not insecurity.
  • Do not default to "founders" unless the source says founder.
  • Never invent proof, status, revenue, or client claims.
07
Process correction

The People page contract.

Going forward, People cannot be only rules in and rules out. That is a gate, not an audience system.

Minimum fields

Required for every brand

  • Strategic audience choice.
  • Primary persona and role context.
  • Psychographics, values, wants, needs, and fears.
  • Business triggers, buying criteria, and objections.
  • Alternatives and wrong comparisons.
  • Image brief, visual exclusions, and AI rules.
  • Proof needed before making claims.
Source discipline

Separate fact from inference

Approved Focus Star language stays the source. Persona and psychographic details are strategic inference until approved. Unknowns should be marked as unknown, not filled with confident copy.
