Section A · Brand Strategy · Edge P2

People.

The Invisible Expert. They have the skill, the experience, and the results. What they lack is a brand that makes all of that visible.

StatusCONFIRMED EdgeP2 PersonaThe Invisible Expert
01
Core Pain

The Invisible Expert.

"I know I'm good at what I do. I just can't explain it in a way that lands."

They have built something real. Clients love the work. But the brand does not reflect the caliber. They keep tweaking the website, rewriting the bio, second-guessing the positioning. Nothing sticks because nothing was built from the inside out.

02
Demographics

Who they are.

Ages 30 to 55. Entrepreneurial by nature. Founders, executives, and solo operators who have outgrown their current brand.

AGE

30 – 55

Mid-career inflection point

MINDSET

Entrepreneurial

Builder mentality, not employee mentality

ROLE

Founder / Executive

Decision-maker with skin in the game

03
Entry Points

Trigger moments.

These are the moments that bring them to the door. Something shifts, and the gap between who they are and how they show up becomes unbearable.

  • Launching a new venture and realizing the old brand does not fit
  • Losing a deal to a less qualified competitor with better positioning
  • Being introduced as something they have outgrown
  • Hitting a revenue ceiling that better visibility would break
  • Preparing for a public moment (keynote, press, funding round)
  • Feeling embarrassed to send people to their own website
04
Client Variants

Three profiles.

The Founder

Solo or small team. Needs a brand that matches the quality of their work. Typically the Brand Therapy for Founders track at $700.

The Executive

Corporate background, transitioning to something of their own. Needs to translate institutional credibility into a personal brand. Often the Founders track first, then Brands.

The Established Brand

Revenue-generating company that has outgrown its original identity. Needs the full system: positioning, messaging, visual direction. The $5K–$15K+ Brand Therapy for Brands track.

05
Fit Filter

Is / Is Not.

Is

  • Self-aware enough to sit in the uncomfortable questions
  • Willing to do the inner work before the outer polish
  • Values clarity over aesthetics
  • Ready to commit, not just explore

Is Not

  • Looking for a quick logo or website facelift
  • Wanting someone to just "make it pretty"
  • Expecting a template they can fill in
  • Shopping for the cheapest option
06
Anti-Persona

Not for them.

The tire-kicker. The person who wants brand strategy to feel like a shopping spree. They want deliverables, not discovery. They want to skip the hard questions and go straight to the font choices.

They will push for a timeline, resist the process, and ultimately need something this is not. Let them go early.
