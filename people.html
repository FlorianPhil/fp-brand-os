People.

FP is built for capable leaders whose value is under-articulated: real expertise, weak articulation, and a visibility gap they are ready to solve.

01
Definition

Who they are.

FP is for founders, executives, and experts with real value that the market cannot yet read fast enough.

02
Tension

What is blocked.

Pain

Hidden value.

"The work is stronger than the way it shows up."

Rational

Opportunity friction.

They need positioning, language, and proof that make expertise legible before a call.

Emotional

Tired of translating.

They want confidence, relief and permission to stop second-guessing.

03
Entry Points

Trigger moments.

Inflection
Visible

A keynote, raise, launch, sale, or new role makes clarity urgent.

Market mismatch
Sharper

Someone less specific is easier to understand, remember, or refer.

Growth ceiling
Clearer

The next level needs clarity, not more effort.

04
Variants

Three doors in.

Founder-led

Trust changes the sale.

The company has value, but the founder's point of view is not doing enough work.

Expert-led

Outcomes outrun the story.

The expertise is real. The language around it still sounds smaller than the work.

New venture

Specific before loud.

They understand brand matters before the market teaches them the expensive way.

05
Fit Filter

Right fit.

Rules in
  • Founders, executives, and experts with real expertise but low public clarity
  • Leaders at inflection moments where visibility now affects opportunity
  • People who want to be understood without being turned into a performance
  • Founder-led or expert-led brands where personal trust changes the sale
  • New ventures that understand brand matters before the market teaches them the expensive way
Rules out
  • Logo shoppers looking for a surface refresh
  • Hustle-culture personal branders optimizing for attention
  • Social media management seekers
  • ROI-first founders who treat brand as a short-term marketing tactic
  • People who want to copy someone louder instead of becoming more specific
06
Tradeoffs

What this choice accepts.

Narrower
Better fit

Narrower than "founders who need branding"; stronger trust, sales clarity, and strategic relevance.

Sharper
Fewer quick fixes

More emotionally precise sales conversations; fewer quick-turn design leads.

Deeper
Less generic

More founder-safe depth; less appeal to clients who only want execution.

Proof required
  • Visible proof that FP has handled founder-led and company brand strategy
  • Case studies showing clarity from ambiguity
  • Testimonials that mention being understood, strategic clarity, positioning, or confidence
Claim boundary
  • Public claims stay limited to verified credentials, case studies, and visible proof assets
  • Do not invent industry authority, revenue outcomes, or client results that are not in the source
  • Use missing proof as a production request, not as copy to improvise around
07
Generation Rules

How AI should use this.

Writing instructions
  • Write to the Invisible Expert as a capable peer, not a beginner
  • Do not describe them as insecure, broken, behind, or naive
  • Tie visibility to opportunity, trust, and articulation, not attention for attention's sake
  • Make the reader feel seen before explaining the offer
  • Keep emotional language precise and adult: relief, confidence, permission to stop tweaking
Image brief
  • Show competence before crisis in a calm strategic environment
  • Frame the subject as observed rather than posed
  • Signal thoughtfulness, restraint, and value that is not fully articulated yet
  • Avoid stock-founder enthusiasm, laptop glow, hustle imagery, shame framing, and young startup cliche
