Section C · Personal Branding

Founder Identity.

The identity principle that governs how Florian shows up across every surface.

SectionC1 StatusConfirmed SpectrumPosition 5
01
Core rule

Host, not star.

Host, not star. Messenger, not guru.

Spotlight rule: Never in the first chair. Spotlight comes indirectly through generosity.

02
Validation

The dinner party test.

Read the copy out loud. If Florian would be uncomfortable saying it at a dinner party with strangers and friends both present, rewrite.

This is the single quality gate for all founder-facing copy. No exceptions. If it sounds like a press release, a LinkedIn bio-hack, or a motivational poster, it fails the test.

03
Application

How it shows.

Six rules that translate the Host/Messenger principle into concrete decisions.

  • The work is about the people Florian serves, not Florian
  • Lead with what he notices about others, not accomplishments
  • No hero framings: no "meet Florian," no "in his own words," no "a letter from me to you"
  • Quotes and testimonials outweigh first-person copy on About-style surfaces
  • When "I" must appear, it serves an observation about someone else or an honest admission
  • Credit influences generously but never put words in their mouths
04
Character

Archetype mix.

Innocent Creator. Optimism + craft. Witty, elegant, real, confident, nuanced.

  • Innocent (50%): Optimism, honesty, belief that clarity is simple when you stop overcomplicating it
  • Creator (30%): Drive to build something meaningful and beautiful
  • Friend (20%): Conversation, not lectures. Perspective shifts, not lessons
05
Essence

Who he is.

Cosmopolitan, grounded, nuanced. European refinement (French) with Californian lightness.

The person at dinner who makes you think and laugh in the same sentence. Shares what he noticed. French words welcome when they capture something English can't.

06
Context

Background.

10+ years branding across 6 countries. Founded 3ANGLES (Taiwan, 25+ clients incl. Acer). CMO in cannabis. Brand Director at 4th Sector Innovations. Mentored by Fabian Geyrhalter.
