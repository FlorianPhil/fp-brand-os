Founder · Brand Spectrum

Where FP sits.

Five positions on a continuum from corporate anonymity to founder-as-the-brand. The spectrum determines how the founder shows up across every surface: website, content, pricing, hiring, exit planning. Test position: 4 (Founder-Forward). FP Position

The founder is the face of the brand. The company name exists, the visual identity supports, but audiences connect with the person first and the entity second.

Content strategy prioritizes the founder's voice. Brand assets amplify rather than replace their presence. Hiring decisions, content production, pricing, and growth bend around the founder's perspective without becoming inseparable from it.

02
Identity Principle

Host, not star.

Position 5 is dangerous without a counterweight. Founder-as-brand can tip into founder-as-protagonist fast. The Host/Messenger principle is the antidote: the founder is everywhere, but never in the first chair.

Core Rule

Host, not star. Messenger, not guru.

Spotlight rule. Never in the first chair. Spotlight comes indirectly through generosity. The work is about the people Florian serves, not Florian.

The dinner party test

Read the copy out loud. If Florian would be uncomfortable saying it at a dinner party with strangers and friends both present, rewrite. Single quality gate for all founder-facing copy. No exceptions.

03
Application

How it shows.

Six rules that translate the Host/Messenger principle into concrete decisions. Every founder-facing surface gets checked against these.

01

The work is about the people Florian serves, not Florian.

02

Lead with what he notices about others, not accomplishments.

03

No hero framings. No "meet Florian," no "in his own words," no "a letter from me to you."

04

Quotes and testimonials outweigh first-person copy on About-style surfaces.

05

When "I" must appear, it serves an observation about someone else or an honest admission.

06

Credit influences generously, but never put words in their mouths.
