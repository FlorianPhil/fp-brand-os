Section B · Brand Identity

Signature.

The visual recognition system. What makes the brand identifiable at a glance, before any text is read.

SectionB3 StatusConfirmed Devices4
01
Foundation

Concept.

Clarity as a visual experience. Things coming into focus, seen through a new lens.

The brand promise (Curiosity / Clarity) is expressed through the viewfinder bracket: a literal visualization of looking through a lens, discovering what was already there. The signature system turns the abstract promise into something people can recognize before they read a single word.

02
Elements

Signature devices.

Viewfinder bracket

Role: Primary signature device. Carries the entire brand recognition.

Two purple L-shaped corners framing the subject. Construction: top-right + bottom-left corners, #5A5CF9, arm length ~30-40% of container, weight 8-10px, sharp 90-degree angles. Everything outside bracket blurred + darkened. Bracket reveals focus within the image.

Full construction details in the Visual Direction page.

Purple dot

Role: Accent mark. Appears at end of headlines (FP.) and as the logo mark.

Color: #5A5CF9. The simplest expression of the brand. Present on every surface where the logo appears. The dot after FP is as much a brand element as the letters themselves.

Grain texture

Role: Subtle warmth layer. 0.03 opacity overlay.

Prevents the design from feeling sterile or clinical. Applied as a fixed overlay across all brand surfaces. Adds the "human touch" that separates the brand from pure Swiss minimalism.

Purple period

Role: Every headline ends with an accent-colored period.

Cheapest brand signal, most consistent. Applied across social posts, website headlines, email headers, and presentation slides. The period is always #5A5CF9. No exceptions on brand-owned surfaces.

03
Benchmark

Recognition test.

If someone sees a photo with two purple L-shaped corners framing the subject, blurred outside, sharp inside, they should think FP before reading any text.

That is the bar. Every visual touchpoint either reinforces this association or dilutes it. There is no neutral. The viewfinder bracket, the purple dot, the grain, and the purple period work together as a system. Remove one, and the recognition weakens. Add a fifth device, and the system gets noisy.
