Section B · Brand Identity

Application.

How strategy and identity translate into repeatable patterns across copy, email, and proof.

SectionB4 StatusConfirmed SourceCollateral factory + Brand Voice
01
Writing

Copy patterns.

Repeatable structures that keep the brand voice consistent without making everything sound identical.

  • Purple dot: Accent-colored period at end of every headline. Cheapest brand signal
  • Two-beat: "Not X. Y." sentence structure. "Not broken. Unfocused." "Not found. Decided."
  • Concrete over abstract: "47 slides" beats "too many slides." "$40K" beats "expensive"
  • We-help avoidance: Never needed. Direct statements always stronger
  • Max headline: 12 words maximum per statement
  • Bold load-bearing: 2-4 bold phrases per section in long-form. Mark what a skim reader needs
02
Email

Email subject patterns.

Pattern bank

  • the tweaking loop
  • what I noticed this week
  • one question
  • the part nobody talks about
  • your about page
  • clarity is decided, not found

Rules

  • Lowercase. No title case
  • Under 6 words preferred
  • Never open with newsletter name or issue number
  • Lead with the idea, not the format
03
Evidence

Proof structure.

Format: Client transformation, not Florian narration. The work speaks through who it served.

Elements

  • Client quote (verbatim when approved)
  • Before/after state
  • Tagline or DNA word as proof of clarity achieved

Case study: Samah Afifi

  • Tagline: "The CTO for startups that want high performance without sacrificing people."
  • DNA: Results With Empathy
  • Archetype: Hero/Caregiver

Case study: Adam Freed

  • Tagline: "The data and operations partner for cannabis leaders who want calm, trusted decisions."
  • DNA: Integrity
  • Archetype: Sage/Ruler
04
Track record

Credibility stack.

Proof points ordered by relevance. Each one demonstrates a specific capability, not just a name.

  • Cachet Advisory: Named, positioned, built from zero
  • 3ANGLES Taiwan: 25+ clients incl. Acer, TAITRA
