Section A · P5 · Personality Intelligence

Personality.

The brand should feel like a smart person noticing the real tension out loud: funny first, diagnostically useful, and restrained enough to stay credible.

Funny first Dry diagnosis Spoken register Innocent · Creator · Friend
Humor is not decoration. It is how the diagnosis lands.
01
Strategic decision

How the brand should behave.

Personality is the behavior layer. It tells AI, designers, copywriters, and Florian what the brand should feel like when strategy becomes expression.

Personality line

Funny first, dry diagnosis

Precise observation with dry humor: warm enough to disarm, sharp enough to be useful, and restrained enough to stay credible.

Voice posture

Observed, not announced

The insight should arrive through the line. The brand does not posture, teach from above, or over-explain its intelligence.

Humor rule

Strategy earns the joke

If the joke works without the strategy point, cut it. Humor should sharpen the diagnosis, not decorate a weak thought.

02
Archetype system

The personality mix.

The archetypes are not a costume. They are a calibration system for how warmth, creation, clarity, and conversational trust should balance.

50% primary

Innocent

Optimism, honesty, and the belief that clarity can feel simple when you stop overcomplicating it.

30% secondary

Creator

The drive to build something meaningful, useful, and visually considered from a real point of view.

20% secondary

Friend

Conversation, not lectures. Perspective shifts, not lessons. A human voice without losing standards.

03
Voice behavior

How it sounds in practice.

These controls should shape social posts, website copy, email, decks, captions, and AI writing prompts.

Sentence posture

Spoken, exact, and slightly dry. The line should feel like Florian noticed the obvious problem everyone politely stepped around.

Insight rhythm

Observation first, diagnosis second, prescription third. Avoid starting with a lesson or a polished thought leadership thesis.

Emotional temperature

Warm snap, not cruelty. Dry, not cold. Useful, not clever for its own sake.

Design behavior

Clean, editorial, restrained, and precise. The visual system should have wit through framing and contrast, not decoration.

04
Rules

What belongs and what does not.

This is the guardrail that prevents the voice from drifting into AI thought leadership, corporate polish, or joke-heavy performance.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Funny first, dry diagnosis.
  • Spoken register.
  • Specific observation before advice.
  • Warm snap, not cruelty.
  • Perspective shift over lesson.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Corporate newsletter voice.
  • Self-help affirmation.
  • Motivational founder content.
  • Joke-only cleverness.
  • Overwritten AI thought leadership.
  • Guru or first-chair founder performance.
05
Marketing use · Creative controls

Proof and output controls.

Personality needs approved examples and negative examples. Without them, AI will overdo the humor or flatten the voice into generic smart copy.

Proof required

Voice evidence

  • Approved writing examples that sound like Florian.
  • Off-voice examples showing where humor or cleverness went too far.
  • Banned phrases and characters enforced in output checks.
AI writing rule

Restraint

  • No em dashes, motivational filler, overworked jokes, or generic thought leadership.
  • Make the insight arrive through the line.
  • Use humor only when it sharpens the diagnosis.
Image rule

Editorial wit

  • Clean, observational, slightly wry.
  • Use framing, contrast, or restraint to create personality.
  • No inspirational stock energy.
06
System contract

The Personality page contract.

Every future brand needs more than a voice adjective. Personality must include behavior, archetype mix, output controls, and examples.

Minimum fields

Required

  • Personality line.
  • Archetype mix.
  • Voice posture.
  • Behavior rules.
  • Rules in and out.
  • Approved and banned examples.
  • AI and image controls.
Replicable rule

Behavior over adjectives

Do not describe the brand as smart, human, funny, premium, or bold without saying how that changes the sentence, the image, the page, or the decision.
