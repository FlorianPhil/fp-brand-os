Section A · P5 · Brand Strategy

Personality.

Funny first, dry diagnosis. The personality is precise observation with dry humor: warm enough to disarm, sharp enough to be useful, and restrained enough to stay credible.

01
Strategic decision

The operating choice.

This is the decision this P owns. Everything downstream should preserve it.

Core decision

Personality

The personality is precise observation with dry humor: warm enough to disarm, sharp enough to be useful, and restrained enough to stay credible.

Interpretation

Plain meaning

The brand sounds like a smart person noticing the true tension out loud, not like a coach, guru, agency, or AI thought leader.

Cost of choice

Tradeoff

This voice will not comfort everyone. It chooses precision, wit, and adult trust over universal warmth.

02
Intelligence layer

What AI must know.

These fields are compact enough for AI context and precise enough for design and marketing decisions.

Personality Line

Funny first, dry diagnosis.

Voice Posture

The brand sounds like a smart person noticing the true tension out loud, not like a coach, guru, agency, or AI thought leader.

Archetype Mix

  • 50% Innocent: optimism, honesty, and clarity that can feel simple.
  • 30% Creator: building something meaningful, useful, and visually considered.
  • 20% Friend: conversation, perspective shifts, and human trust.

Voice Behavior

  • Spoken, exact, and slightly dry.
  • Observation first, diagnosis second, prescription third.
  • Warm snap, not cruelty.
  • Clean, editorial, restrained visual behavior.

Creative Controls

  • Clean, observational, slightly wry imagery.
  • Use framing, contrast, or restraint to create personality.
  • Avoid inspirational stock energy, joke-only cleverness, and guru performance.
03
Rules

What belongs and what does not.

A real strategy excludes things. These are the hard filters for copy, design, content, and sales.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Funny first, dry diagnosis.
  • Spoken register.
  • Specific observation before advice.
  • Warm snap, not cruelty.
  • Perspective shift over lesson.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Corporate newsletter voice.
  • Self-help affirmation.
  • Motivational founder content.
  • Joke-only cleverness.
  • Overwritten AI thought leadership.
  • Guru or first-chair founder performance.
04
Marketing use

Triggers, proof, and output controls.

This is the AI-readable layer for website copy, landing pages, sales, content, visual direction, and image prompts.

Triggers

When it matters

  • Humor is not decoration. It is how the diagnosis lands. If the joke works without the strategy point, cut it.
Proof required

Show evidence

  • Approved writing examples that sound like Florian.
  • Off-voice examples showing where humor or cleverness went too far.
  • Banned phrases and characters enforced in output checks.
AI rule

AI behavior

No em dashes, no motivational filler, no overworked jokes, no generic thought leadership.

Output control

Copy

Make the insight arrive through the line. Keep humor functional and sparse.

Output control

Visual

Sharp, clean, restrained, with a viewfinder or diagnostic feel.

Output control

Image

Clean, editorial, observational, slightly wry. No inspirational stock energy.
