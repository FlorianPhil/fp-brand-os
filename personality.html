Section A · Brand Strategy · Edge P5

Personality.

Funny first, dry diagnosis: the absurdity inside the truth, warm enough to disarm, sharp enough to be useful.

StatusCONFIRMED EdgeP5
Center
Funny first Humor is how insight arrives.
Function
Diagnosis Name the tension plainly.
Temperature
Warm snap Disarming, not soft.
Rule
Cut the joke If it works without strategy.
01
Definition

What it is.

The voice finds the absurdity inside the truth. It observes before prescribing, names the specific tension and leaves a perspective shift.

02
Voice

How it sounds.

Humor

Funny first.

The joke sharpens the point. It does not decorate it.

Diagnosis

Dry.

Name the tension. Let the absurdity sit there.

Pace

Unhurried.

Short to anchor. Longer when the thought needs breath.

03
Archetype Mix

The blend.

Primary
50%

Innocent. Optimism, honesty, clarity without overcomplication.

Edge
30%

Creator. Drive to build something meaningful and beautiful.

Warmth
20%

Friend. Conversation, not lectures. Perspective shifts, not lessons.

04
Fit Filter

Is / Is not.

Voice is
  • Funny first
  • Dry diagnosis
  • Warm snap
  • Observant, specific, curious, conversational
Voice is not
  • Generic coaching-speak
  • Self-help affirmation
  • Corporate newsletter
  • Joke-only cleverness
