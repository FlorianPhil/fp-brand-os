Section A · P4 · Promise Intelligence

Promise.

Curiosity is the operating standard: look closely before deciding, ask the question that reveals what is actually there, and turn uncertainty into sharper choices.

Curiosity Diagnosis Questions first Earned clarity
The promise is not certainty. It is the discipline that earns clarity.
01
Strategic decision

What every decision must hold.

Promise is the behavioral standard. It should shape discovery, writing, visual metaphor, sales calls, content, and AI generation whenever the source is incomplete.

Promise name

Curiosity

Curiosity keeps Brand Therapy from becoming templates, taste theater, borrowed positioning, or confident answers around the wrong problem.

Client experience

Accurately seen

The client should feel that the work noticed what others skipped: the hidden tension, the wrong comparison, the real source of value.

Operating tension

Open, not vague

Curiosity is not endless exploration. It is disciplined looking that turns uncertainty into a better question, then a sharper decision.

02
Behavioral system

How Curiosity behaves.

Promise becomes useful when it changes how the brand asks, writes, presents, and decides.

Discovery

Ask before asserting. Look for the contradiction, the avoided choice, the wrong comparison, and the detail that explains why the obvious answer is too flat.

Writing

Lead with observation. Use plain diagnosis. If something is uncertain, say what is uncertain instead of filling the gap with confident brand jargon.

Design

Use frames, annotations, evidence, focus, and proximity. Design should feel like looking closer, not adding noise.

AI generation

When source data is missing, the system should flag the gap, ask for evidence, or state an inference. It should not invent strategic certainty.

03
Rules

What belongs and what does not.

This keeps Curiosity from becoming either vague exploration or performative cleverness.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Questions before assertions.
  • Observation before prescription.
  • Diagnosis before style.
  • Specific tension before broad advice.
  • Decisions that reveal what is already there.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Performing certainty to sound senior.
  • Best-practice answers before looking closely.
  • Borrowed category language.
  • Copying someone else's brand logic.
  • Jokes or taste choices that replace the strategy point.
04
Marketing use

Proof and trust logic.

Promise should show why Florian is trusted: not because he performs certainty, but because he can see what is actually happening.

Proof required

Show the question

  • Examples where a better question changed the strategy.
  • Before/after showing a hidden tension becoming the core position.
  • Client language that mentions feeling seen, understood, or accurately diagnosed.
Content prompts

What to write about

  • The suspiciously obvious answer.
  • The question nobody asked before designing.
  • The tiny detail that explains the whole positioning problem.
AI rule

State uncertainty

  • When uncertain, ask or state the uncertainty.
  • Do not fill gaps with confident brand jargon.
  • Separate approved source from strategic inference.
05
Creative controls

How Promise should look and sound.

Curiosity should feel precise, observant, and useful. It should not feel whimsical, mystical, academic, or uncertain for its own sake.

Image brief

Close looking

  • Frames, notes, margins, evidence, diagrams, or diagnostic moments.
  • Visual cues of attention, not decoration.
  • Quiet inspection before conclusion.
Avoid visually

No insight cliches

  • No crystal balls, lightbulbs, binoculars, or generic eye icons.
  • No mystical fog or magical reveal.
  • No consultant certainty theater.
Promise contract

Minimum fields

  • Operating standard.
  • Client experience.
  • Behavioral rules.
  • Discovery and writing rules.
  • Proof required.
  • AI uncertainty protocol.
  • Image exclusions.
