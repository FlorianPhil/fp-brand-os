Section A · Brand Strategy · P4

Promise.

Curiosity is what keeps Brand Therapy from becoming templates, taste theater, or borrowed positioning. It is the method that finds the real point of view.

Curiosity

Curiosity is the operating promise: look closely before deciding, ask the question that reveals what is actually there, and turn uncertainty into sharper choices.

Strategic question

Strategic question

  • What standard should every decision hold?
Use

Use

  • Questions before assertions.
  • Observation before prescription.
  • Diagnosis before style.
  • Specific tension before broad advice.
  • Decisions that reveal what is already there.
Avoid

Avoid

  • Performing certainty to sound senior.
  • Best-practice answers before looking closely.
  • Borrowed category language.
  • Copying someone else's brand logic.
  • Jokes or taste choices that replace the strategy point.
Proof required

Proof required

  • Examples where a better question changed the strategy.
  • Before/after showing a hidden tension becoming the core position.
  • Client language that mentions feeling seen, understood, or accurately diagnosed.
AI rule

AI rule

  • When uncertain, ask or state the uncertainty. Do not fill gaps with confident brand jargon.
Image signal

Image signal

  • Close looking, notes, frames, or a moment of diagnosis. No crystal ball, lightbulb, or generic insight icon.
