Section B · Brand Identity

Visual Direction.

Clarity as a visual experience. Things coming into focus, seen through a new lens.

SectionB2 StatusConfirmed ThemeLight EdgesSharp only
01
Foundation

Core concept.

Clarity as a visual experience. Things coming into focus, seen through a new lens.

  • Theme: Light-forward. White/light background default. Dark surfaces only as inverted accent sections, never the default canvas
  • Edges: Sharp. All zero border-radius. No curves, no rounded corners
  • Density: Extreme whitespace
  • Reference aesthetic: Editorial dossier, not SaaS dashboard
02
Signature device

The viewfinder bracket.

Two purple L-shaped corners framing the subject. The literal visualization of looking through a lens.

Construction

  • Corners: Top-right + bottom-left
  • Color: #5A5CF9
  • Arm length: ~30-40% of container dimension
  • Weight: 8-10px relative to photo container
  • Angles: Sharp 90-degree. No curves, no rounded corners

Focus treatment

Everything outside bracket blurred + darkened. Bracket reveals focus within the image. No separate dark background.

Tiers

  • Primary: Full bracket with blur/darken. Hero usage on social, blog thumbnails, email headers
  • Ambient: Bracket only (no blur), reduced opacity or partial corners. Website accents, email footers

Variants

  • Hero overlay: 2-corner (top-right + bottom-left). Full blur + darken outside bracket. Hero images, social post primary photos, blog thumbnails
  • Heading lockup: Top-left only. Asymmetric L: top arm extending right, short left arm down. border-top: 2px solid #5A5CF9; border-left: 2px solid #5A5CF9; width: 28px; height: 10px. Eyebrow + headline lockups
  • Ambient: Partial or reduced-opacity bracket. No blur. Website accents, email footers, presentation slides
  • Button: 4-corner. Buttons and interactive elements only

Critical: hero_overlay = 2-corner. Buttons = 4-corner. Do not swap.

03
Palette

Colors.

Purple is always accent, never background. Exception: carousel slides, presentation decks. No warm colors ever.

  • Accent: #5A5CF9
  • Accent glow: rgba(90, 92, 249, 0.35)
  • Accent soft: rgba(90, 92, 249, 0.06)
  • Text: #1A1A2E
  • Muted: #8888A0
  • Border: #E5E7EB
  • Light: #F5F5F7
  • White: #FFFFFF
  • Dark: #18181B
  • Dark surface: #1D1D1D
04
Type system

Typography.

Display: Bricolage Grotesque

  • Weights: 200, 300, 400, 600, 700, 800
  • Default weight: 800
  • Letter spacing: -0.02em
  • Line height: 1.05

Body: Poppins

  • Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
  • Default weight: 400
  • Line height: 1.7

Content rules

  • Headlines: Bricolage Grotesque, bold/800, ALL CAPS
  • Supporting: Poppins, regular/medium, sentence case
  • Purple period: Mandatory at end of hook on every post. #5A5CF9
  • Single word highlight: One word per hook emphasized (bold, purple, or size shift). Never more than one
  • Footer attribution: Poppins, small, all caps, letter-spacing. For #BRAND THERAPY WEEKLY

No third typeface. Two families only: Bricolage Grotesque + Poppins. Mono-style labels use Poppins at letter-spacing 0.16-0.20em. No third family ever.

05
Imagery

Photography.

AI-generated for both modes. Exception: real speaking/stage photos (can be AI-enhanced). Consistent lighting direction, color grading, surface language across all images. Same photographer feel.

Editorial default: Light color overall. Light bases, soft tones, restrained shadows. #5A5CF9 as restrained accent only.

Personal mode

When Florian is in the frame. Natural/available light, warm color temperature. Full color inside bracket.

  • Strategist: Dark button-down shirt, sleeves rolled up, no tie
  • Speaker: Checkered shirt under vest, sleeves rolled up. No pocket square, no handkerchief
  • Thinker: Dark crew neck sweater or tee, minimal

Composition: one idea per post. Oversized bold typography + photo inside viewfinder bracket.

Subject mode

Concept, object, or metaphor. No Florian. Industrial minimalism. Matte textures, concrete/marble/stone, studio directional lighting. Japanese clean minimalism with slight organic warmth.

  • Purple injection: ~3/4 images use #5A5CF9 as selective color accent within the subject. ~1/4 fully neutral, bracket alone carries brand
  • Primary use: BTW blog thumbnails, newsletter headers. Adaptable to square for social
  • Safe subjects: Concrete, glass, metal objects render well in AI. Animals and organic textures need careful prompting
06
Mark

Logo.

  • Mark: FP.
  • Placement: Bottom-left
  • Style: White with purple dot
  • Size: Small, consistent
  • URL line: florianp.com bottom-right, subtle sans-serif, ~1 in 3 posts
07
System

Spacing and motion.

  • Container: 1200px
  • Gutter clamp: clamp(24px, 5vw, 64px)
  • Section padding: clamp(100px, 12vw, 180px)
  • Border radius: 0. All zero. Sharp edges only

Motion curves

  • Ease out expo: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
  • Ease out quart: cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)
  • Ease in-out quint: cubic-bezier(0.86, 0, 0.07, 1)
  • Grain opacity: 0.03
08
Avoid

Anti-patterns.

If a design direction matches any of these, it's wrong. Stop and rethink.

  • Magazine cover metaphors
  • Glass panels or glassmorphism as brand concept
  • Prism, crystal, 3D refraction imagery
  • B&W with selective color reveal (old signature, replaced by viewfinder)
  • Polaroid treatments
  • Avatar-size photography
  • Generic stock photography
  • Rounded pill buttons
  • Multiple accent colors
  • Decorative elements for decoration's sake
  • Dark canvas as default (light-forward brand)

Iconography: Strict minimalism. Thin-line, one or two colors only. No fills. Photography carries emotional weight.
