08
System · Constraints

Design Rules.

Non-negotiable constraints. These rules exist to maintain visual coherence across every touchpoint.

01
Sharp edges

Zero border-radius everywhere. Precision is the aesthetic.

02
Light canvas

White is the default surface. Dark sections used sparingly as accent.

03
Extreme whitespace

Generous spacing between sections. Density is the enemy of clarity.

04
Single accent

One accent color carries brand energy. No secondary accent.

05
No warm colors

Reds, oranges, yellows permanently excluded. Cool palette only.

06
Two typefaces

Display + body. No third typeface. Mono-style labels use the body family with letter-spacing.

07
Grain texture

Subtle noise overlay on all surfaces. Analog warmth against digital precision.

08
Photography-forward

Real images, not illustrations. Slightly desaturated, high contrast.
