02
Type system

Typography.

Two typefaces. Bricolage Grotesque for impact. Poppins for information.

Display
Bricolage Grotesque

Weight: 800 (ExtraBold). Use: hero headlines, section titles, pull quotes. ALL CAPS for hero-level type. Accent-colored period at the end of every brand headline.

Body
Poppins

Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold. Use: body text, labels, navigation, UI. Mono-style labels = same Poppins, tracked out 0.16–0.20em.

03
System · Rules

Do & Don't.

Non-negotiable. These rules exist to maintain visual coherence across every touchpoint.

Do

Use the purple period at the end of every brand headline

Do

Frame photos with the viewfinder bracket (2-corner on photos: top-right + bottom-left)

Do

Lead with whitespace

Do

Bold the load-bearing phrases (2-4 per long-form section)

Do

Use sharp 90-degree corners on every element

Don't

Use em-dashes (U+2014) or en-dashes (U+2013) in public paragraph copy (headlines, hero sentences, body prose, hooks). OK in lists, quotes, parentheticals, file comments, and background context.

Don't

Round any corners

Don't

Introduce a third typeface

Don't

Use warm colors anywhere

Don't

Use polaroid treatments or stock photography

Don't

Use light font weights for display

Don't

Use italic emphasis on display type

Don't

Use dark canvas as default

04
Imagery · Photography

Photography.

How real photos and AI-generated imagery should look.

Source: AI-generated for both personal and subject modes. Exception: real speaking/stage photos (can be AI-enhanced but not AI-generated).

Quality standard: Consistent lighting direction, color grading, and surface language across all images. Every image looks like the same photographer made it.

Modes:

  • Personal mode (Florian in frame):
    • Style: natural/available light, warm color temperature. Full color inside the viewfinder bracket.
    • Outfits:
      • Strategist: dark button-down shirt, sleeves rolled up, no tie
      • Speaker: checkered shirt under vest, sleeves rolled up. No pocket square, no handkerchief
      • Thinker: dark crew neck sweater or tee, minimal
    • Composition: one idea per post. Oversized bold typography + photo inside viewfinder bracket.
  • Subject mode (concept, object, metaphor: no Florian):
    • Style: industrial minimalism. Matte textures, concrete/marble/stone, studio directional lighting. Japanese clean minimalism with slight organic warmth.
    • Purple injection: ~3/4 of images use #5A5CF9 as selective color accent within the subject. ~1/4 fully neutral; the bracket alone carries the brand.
    • Primary use: BTW blog thumbnails, newsletter headers. Adaptable to square for social.

Safe subjects (render well in AI): concrete, glass, metal objects.

Risky subjects (need careful prompting): animals, organic textures, intricate fabrics.

Forbidden imagery:

  • Staged power poses
  • Arms crossed
  • Gradient backdrops
  • Over-styled environments
  • Generic stock photography
  • Spa, therapy, or wellness cues (would conflict with the Brand Therapy methodology naming rule)

Editorial default: light color overall. Light bases, soft tones, restrained shadows. #5A5CF9 as restrained accent only, never the dominant color in the frame.

05
Categories the brand refuses

Anti-references.

What this brand is visually and categorically NOT, framed as anti-targets for AI image generation, layout work, and brand positioning.

Consultancy template aesthetics

Heavy gradient hero blocks, stock-photo people in front of glass walls, "trusted by" carousels of bland logos. FP's editorial restraint is the opposite.

Personal-brand influencer aesthetics

Cluttered LinkedIn graphics, neon accents, motivational quote overlays, branded talking-head selfies. FP is observed, not performed.

Spa, therapy, or wellness aesthetics

Soft warm gradients, sans-serif feminine type, leaf icons, "healing journey" language. The "therapy" in Brand Therapy is psychology of branding, not healing.

SaaS-dashboard aesthetics

Utility-first density, neutral grays, data-table layouts, "feature" grids with checkmarks. FP is editorial, not utility.

Logo-portfolio studio aesthetics

Lead with mark, big mark grids, monogram-as-hero, "brand systems" portfolio pages. FP leads with strategy, not the mark.

AI-content-mill aesthetics

Generic centered hero, three feature columns, listicle-style content blocks, "AI-powered" claims. FP shows precision, not output volume.
