Section C · Personal Branding

Imagery.

The visual and physical presence rules that govern how Florian appears in photos, on stage, and in deliverables.

SectionC3 StatusConfirmed SourceVisual Identity + Brand Voice
01
Energy

Presence.

  • Energy: The person at dinner who makes you think and laugh in the same sentence
  • Posture: Relaxed, open, assured. Never performative or staged
  • Eye contact: Direct but not intense. Observes more than locks on
  • Listening style: Notes patterns. Asks the question that reframes the conversation
02
Dress code

Wardrobe.

Palette

Navy, charcoal, black, white. Clean neutrals only.

Silhouettes

Clean, unfussy. European tailoring without the stiffness.

Signature pieces

  • Strategist: Dark button-down, sleeves rolled up
  • Speaker: Checkered shirt under vest, sleeves rolled up
  • Thinker: Dark crew neck or tee

Forbidden

  • Suits that look like they're wearing you
  • Pocket squares, handkerchiefs, ties
  • Logo-forward clothing
  • Hustle-bro accessories
03
Photography

Aesthetic.

Portrait style

Editorial. Natural/available light. Warm color temperature. Not posed, not candid. Observed.

Settings

  • Industrial minimal spaces (concrete, glass, matte surfaces)
  • Atrium or window light
  • Working spaces (desk, whiteboard, notebook)

No-go list

  • Staged power poses
  • Arms crossed
  • Looking away meaningfully
  • Gradient backdrops
  • Over-styled environments
04
Delivery

Presentation.

Deliverable format

Sparse slides. Heavy whitespace. One idea per slide. Bricolage Grotesque headlines.

Energy in presenting

Conversational keynote energy. Thinks out loud with structure. Checks in with "right?" as invitation.

Closing style

Ends with a perspective shift, not a call to action.
